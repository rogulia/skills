# SEO & GEO optimization skill for AI agents

An [Agent Skill](https://agentskills.io/specification) that gives your AI agent (Claude Code, Codex and others) the
current rules for being crawled, indexed, ranked and **cited**, in classic search engines and in generative AI
answer engines.

**Why it's different**
- **First-party only.** Built from about 190 pages of official documentation of the major search and AI answer
  engines, verified on 2026-09-25. Every rule is tagged with the documentation set that states it.
- **No folklore.** Fixed title lengths, word counts, "FAQ schema for AI" and "llms.txt as a ranking lever" are
  listed as unsupported, with the reason.
- **Engine-neutral.** It says where engines agree and where they differ, and how to act when they do.
- **Operational.** Bot and crawler control (search vs training vs user-triggered), a finding format, evidence rules,
  and a change lifecycle for audits.

**Contents:** `SKILL.md` holds the core rules and the index. `references/` holds the details: bots and robots.txt,
index and AI controls, canonicals and redirects, sitemaps and rendering, international, content, spam, appearance and
structured data, commerce and agents, measurement and diagnostics, myths, and the audit procedure. There is also an
engine reference with crawler tokens and a source list.
